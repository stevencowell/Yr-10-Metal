# Year 10 Metalwork guided course

This is the Year 10 sibling to the established Year 9 Metal guided course. It follows the supplied Year 10 Metal 1 and Metal Machining 2 programs through two projects:

1. BBQ and Case
2. Folding Camping Shovel

The current 2026 Year 10 assessment handbook controls formal task timing and weighting. Older project documents remain evidence and content sources only. Teacher-issued drawings, briefs, SOPs, WMS templates and demonstrations control all project dimensions, materials, tolerances, machine settings, safety actions and approved modifications.

Student responses autosave locally in the browser and can be printed or backed up. Local browser storage is evidence preparation, not automatic submission.

## Formal assessment status

- Task 1: Project 1 and Folio · Term 2 Week 8 · 45%
- Task 2: Project 2 and Folio · Term 3 Week 10 · 35%
- Task 3: Practical/theory examination · Term 4 Week 3 · 20%

The notices remain clearly labelled as drafts wherever teacher-controlled details are not supplied. The formal exam stays locked until an approved classroom password is entered in `guided/exam-config.js`.

## Validation

Run `node scripts/validate-course.mjs` after generating the course data with `node scripts/build-course-data.mjs`.
