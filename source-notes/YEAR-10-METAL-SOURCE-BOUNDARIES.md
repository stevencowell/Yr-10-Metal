# Year 10 Metal — exact source-boundary manifest

## Controlling source hierarchy

1. 2026 Wagga Wagga High School Year 10 assessment handbook schedule.
2. Google Drive Metal folder and its Year 10 program, assessment, exam and teaching-sequence documents.
3. NSW Industrial Technology 7–10 (2019) Stage 5 outcome wording and the supplied Metal 1 / Metal Machining 2 syllabus extracts.
4. Teacher-issued drawings, SOPs and demonstrations for exact project geometry, dimensions, machine setup and task-specific safety.

Older project sheets may support evidence requirements and project context, but historical dates or weightings do not override the current handbook. Exact issue/calendar dates, submission details, teacher name and component marks remain **Teacher to confirm** unless supplied by an authoritative current source.

## Section contracts


### 1.1 Reading the BBQ-and-case brief as a production contract

- **Source basis:** Authoritative Scope, BBQ and Case evidence list; teacher-issued project brief and drawings; Stage 5 Industrial Technology process expectations.
- **Project/workshop context:** Students are beginning Project 1 and need to translate the approved brief into a folio checklist before practical production.
- **Non-invention boundary:** Do not state dimensions, stock sizes, materials, heat source, capacity, construction details, finish system or component marks unless directly supplied in the live prompt from an authoritative document.
- **Linked knowledge-check concept:** Classify an example as an authoritative requirement, evidence requirement or design preference.
- **Written-evidence concept:** Create a short requirements-and-evidence table that links one confirmed brief requirement to the proof the student will collect.
