# Verified Year 10 Metal project plans

## Authority and non-invention boundary

These files were recovered from Steve's supplied Metal Google Drive source tree and visually inspected before integration. They are core teaching sources, not decorative images. Website previews may be resized and the shovel previews are rotated for readability, but the source drawing content is not cropped, redrawn or altered.

The current teacher-issued drawings, project brief, SOPs and demonstrations remain authoritative. The website does not infer missing dimensions, materials, tolerances, fits, threads, finishes, settings, construction sequences, safety steps or approved modifications.
