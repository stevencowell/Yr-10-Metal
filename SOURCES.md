## Authorship and visual provenance

- Each of the 42 named theory sections was authored separately through signed-in ChatGPT from a source-bounded brief, then integrated without adding unsupported project detail.
- New course visuals were created separately through signed-in ChatGPT and visually checked. They are illustrative evidence prompts, not project drawings.
- Examination stimuli are recovered originals from the supplied examination document; they are not redrawn.
- The verified BBQ and Case and Folding Camping Shovel drawing sets are integrated beside their matching theory. Original DWGs/PDFs are preserved, website previews are not cropped, and the shovel previews are rotated only for readability.
- The two plan-specific guidance additions were authored separately through signed-in ChatGPT in the in-app browser, then integrated into existing sections 2.2 and 10.1 so the authoritative 42-section/420-question structure remains unchanged.
