Course home

Browser autosave and backup files help you prepare evidence. They do not submit work to your teacher.

Project brief and function

Summarise the need, intended user, function and teacher-issued requirements.

Requirements and modifications

Separate requirements from constraints and explain any teacher-approved modification.

WHS and risk controls

Record hazards, risk level, stronger controls, PPE and teacher permission points.

Materials investigation

Compare relevant metals or stock forms and justify the teacher-specified selection.

Research and ideas

Record research, freehand sketches or modification ideas and explain the approved direction.

Working drawing evidence

Explain how the teacher-issued drawing communicates material, dimensions, shape, tolerance or assembly information.

Cutting, costing and resources

Summarise your spreadsheet list and explain one decision that reduced waste or improved efficiency.

Production plan and WMS

Sequence key processes, evidence checkpoints, demonstrations and approvals.

Production evidence

Write captions for your own photographs that explain the process, control and result shown.

Quality and tolerance checks

Record the approved criteria, measurement or fit evidence, result and any correction.

Industry and impacts

Compare one classroom and industrial process, then explain a societal or environmental impact.

PMI and final evaluation

Use plus, minus and interesting observations to judge function, quality and process, then identify one realistic improvement.

Keep evidence specific, honest and connected to your own project work.
