Task outline

Produce the teacher-authorised Folding Camping Shovel and a folio documenting planning, machining and fabrication evidence, safe production, communication, quality control and evaluation. Teacher-issued drawings, demonstrations and local procedures control all geometry, dimensions, materials, fits, tolerances, threads, finishes, processes and settings.

Required evidence

  • Teacher-observed safe work practices and completed practical project.
  • Workshop drawings, metal-lathe SOP evidence and construction WMS.
  • Spreadsheet cutting and costing list.
  • Progressive, captioned production evidence where authorised.
  • Societal and environmental impact report.
  • PMI evaluation and completed project report/folio.

Assessed outcomes and marking criteria

The program identifies IND5-1 to IND5-10. The exact assessed subset, formal wording, component marks and final rubric are Teacher to confirm.

  1. Safe work practices and risk management.
  2. Planning, design communication and project management.
  3. Materials, tools, equipment and processes.
  4. Practical quality and confirmed requirements.
  5. Production evidence and technical communication.
  6. Industry, societal and environmental understanding.
  7. Evaluation and improvement.
